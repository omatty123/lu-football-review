The evidence behind the graphs

Official conference records and school boxscores. Completed games only. Every team is compared over 2015–2019 and 2021–2025; rates are weighted by games.

Sources, definitions, and coverage
  • Comparison group: the ten 2025 MWC teams, including Chicago’s 2015–2016 seasons before it joined. Full 2011–2025 comparison, former members, and membership-period totals remain in the full report.
  • Loss rate: losses ÷ all completed games. SUPER LOSS rate: qualifying losses ÷ all completed games, including wins in the denominator. Opponent total touchdowns must be strictly greater than the losing team’s first downs. Equality does not count.
  • Scoring margin: total points scored minus total points allowed, divided by games. All points count; this is not a pure measure of the defense. Opponent strength is not adjusted.
  • Unequal schedules: canceled games are excluded. Grinnell played three games in 2019 and eight in 2022. No team played a 2020 MWC season.
  • Audit coverage: The underlying 2011–2025 audit checked all 761 defeats and found 54 SUPER LOSSES. These graphs use only the ten selected seasons. Scoring-summary checks agreed in 759 cases. Two malformed summaries were resolved using team-statistics totals; neither game qualifies as a SUPER LOSS. Row-level notes are in the audit data.
